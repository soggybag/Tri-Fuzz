Tri-Fuzz v0.3: simulated clips

ngspice model of the whole signal path (behavioural TL07x-like op-amps, 1N4148 / red LED diode models) fed with a synthetic guitar (Karplus-Strong). The main players go through a rough 1×12 cab filter; "DI" is the raw pedal output. All clips are loudness-matched so you compare tone; the last column is the real output level relative to clip 02. Caveats: the synthetic guitar is darker than a real pickup (so HIGH is under-represented), and noise is not simulated.
